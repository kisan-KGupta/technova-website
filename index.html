WAREHOUSE & LOGISTICS SAFETY CASE STUDY

Challenging Dismissive Supervisors in High-Risk Walkthroughs

THE CLIENT CHALLENGE:

Junior warehouse trainees routinely noticed safety hazards (oil leaks, blocked fire routes) but failed to escalate them due to authority deference when walking with dismissive veteran supervisors.

THE TECHNOVA SOLUTION:

We built a zero-install 3D desktop simulation featuring dynamic NPC supervisor *Dave*. The simulator places trainees in interactive walkthroughs where they must diagnose hazards and actively push back against dismissive advice.

THE SAFETY EVOLUTION

Beyond Boring Slides. Beyond Heavy VR Goggles.

Why forward-thinking safety leaders are replacing passive checkboxes and clunky headsets with lightweight, browser-native 3D simulations.

PASSIVE CHECKBOXES

Legacy Slide LMS

"Passive completion without real-world retention."

Static slides test multiple-choice recall rather than decision reflexes. Trainees memorize right answers for the test, but quickly defer to unsafe supervisor shortcuts on the actual warehouse floor.

  • 70% knowledge decay within 48 hours
  • Zero behavioral authority testing
  • Passive click-through engagement
Fails Under Real Pressure
HARDWARE-BOUND VR

Proprietary VR Goggles

"High spatial depth trapped behind hardware friction."

Dedicated headsets offer visual immersion but fail operational scalability. Storing, charging, sanitizing, and troubleshooting expensive hardware creates friction that keeps devices locked in IT closets.

  • High hardware capex and upkeep
  • Battery, cleaning & pairing bottlenecks
  • Motion discomfort for distributed teams

STUDENT ENTERPRISE · CET257 TASK B2

Cognitive Science Meets Spatial Computing

Formed at the University of Sunderland for the CET257 Enterprise Project, TechNova bridges the critical gap between traditional regulatory compliance and real-world psychological decision-making on high-risk industrial floors.

Our Enterprise Mission

To reduce preventable workplace accidents by engineering accessible, browser-first 3D simulations that actively test and dismantle authority bias, risk normalization, and complacency before employees step onto active operational floors.

Our Core Value Pillars

  • Zero Hardware Barriers: Universal WebGL delivery requiring zero expensive VR headsets or local IT installs.
  • Behavioral Fidelity: Rooted in HCI and Human Factors research to model nuanced interpersonal pressure.
  • Academic Integrity: Developed under University of Sunderland CET257 assessment criteria to demonstrate rigorous software engineering and marketing communication.
